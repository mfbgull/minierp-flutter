# Unapplied customer cash UI: F6 payment-surface integration

Status: F6 complete. Plan: `.omo/plans/unapplied-customer-cash-ui.md`.

## Delivered

- Payment kind parsing retains RECEIPT compatibility when the legacy field is
  absent. Shared presentation labels distinguish Receipt, Advance receipt and
  Customer refund. Unknown kinds fail closed for generic modification.
- Customer payment history labels kinds and colors refunds as cash outflows,
  retaining their signed amount. The unified hub uses its persisted positive
  amount plus direction; refund rows show a negative cash outflow.
- Advance/refund details are read-only with a supported-corrections explanation
  and Print Receipt. Ordinary receipts retain permission-gated edit/delete.
  Direct generic editor construction is also guarded. Customer row menus remove
  unsupported edit/delete; the actions button supports keyboard activation.
- Bulk deletion is disabled for selections containing only protected documents.
  Mixed selections dispatch ordinary receipt deletions only; protected documents
  become localized exclusions, never calls to the generic DELETE endpoint.
- Reprints fetch the saved payment with GET before invoking the existing print
  service. No creation or idempotency request is generated by printing. A4 and
  thermal templates distinguish advance/refund titles and Paid To/Amount Paid.
  They show absolute cash amounts and omit invented invoice-AR balance trails or
  allocations for new document kinds. The existing English PDF convention remains.
- CSV exports include localized kind and explicit cash direction, with signed
  computed cash amounts. Applications remain cash activity, not new receipts;
  desktop/narrow activity invoice buttons open the existing invoice preview.
- Successful cash operations invalidate open payment details as well as the hub
  and customer consumers. Payment grids remount locally on locale/theme changes,
  keeping shared grid internals untouched. The hub selection column has an 80px
  minimum to accommodate PlutoGrid's RTL checkbox/title layout.
- Eleven English/Urdu keys and generated localizations reuse existing theme,
  typography, status and spacing primitives. Detail footer actions wrap.
